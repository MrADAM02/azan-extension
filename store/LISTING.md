## Detailed description – English

```
Azan Tab turns every new tab into a calm prayer-times dashboard, and reminds you of each prayer with the azan.

PRAYER TIMES
• Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for your city, with a live countdown to the next prayer
• Calculated on your device, so times work offline
• 12 calculation methods (Umm al-Qura, Egyptian, Muslim World League, ISNA, Karachi, Dubai, Kuwait, Qatar, Singapore, Turkey, Tehran, Moonsighting Committee); the official method for your country is picked automatically
• Hanafi or standard Asr, high-latitude rules, and per-prayer minute adjustments to match your local mosque

AZAN AND REMINDERS
• Built-in azan recorded at the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah, full or short, or a gentle chime
• Upload your own azan, and a separate one for Fajr
• Notifications for each prayer, and a reminder a few minutes before
• Jumu'ah reminder and a Friday reminder to read Surat al-Kahf
• Countdown to the next prayer on the toolbar icon

RAMADAN
• Iftar and Suhoor countdown with the fasting progress
• Imsak time and reminders before Suhoor ends

MORE
• Hijri date (Umm al-Qura), adjustable by up to 2 days
• Monthly timetable in Gregorian or Hijri months; print it or export to CSV
• A Quran verse, morning and evening adhkar, or a hadith on each new tab
• Qibla direction and current weather
• English and Arabic, light and dark themes, 12 or 24-hour clock, backgrounds that follow the time of day, or your own image
• Quick popup from the toolbar with today's times and a button to stop the azan

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location and settings stay in your browser. Weather and city search use Open-Meteo; the city name for a detected location comes from BigDataCloud. Privacy policy: https://github.com/MrADAM02/azan-extension/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

Quran text: Tanzil.net (CC BY 3.0). English translation: Saheeh International. Azan recording: ejaz215 via Freesound (CC BY 3.0).
```

## Chrome Web Store – Privacy practices tab

**Single purpose:**
Shows Islamic prayer times for the user's location on the New Tab page and toolbar popup, and alerts the user at each prayer time with a notification and the azan.

**Permission justifications:**

| Permission | Justification |
|---|---|
| storage | Saves the user's location and settings. |
| alarms | Schedules the notification and azan at each prayer time, even when no extension page is open. |
| notifications | Shows a notification at each prayer time and optional reminders before prayers, Jumu'ah, Suhoor and Iftar. |
| offscreen | Plays the azan audio, because the background service worker cannot play audio. |
| geolocation | Used only when the user clicks "Detect my location", to find their coordinates for prayer times. City search is available instead. |
| Host: api.open-meteo.com | Current weather for the user's city, shown on the New Tab. |
| Host: geocoding-api.open-meteo.com | City search when the user sets their location. |
| Host: api.bigdatacloud.net | Turns detected coordinates into a city name to display. |
| New Tab override | The extension's main feature is a prayer-times dashboard on the New Tab page. |

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code. (All scripts are bundled.)

**Data usage – what user data do you collect?** Tick **Location**. It is used to calculate prayer times; coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo for weather, and to BigDataCloud for the city name. Nothing else is collected.

**Certify all three:**
- I do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, outside of the approved use cases.
- I do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to my item's single purpose.
- I do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
